What CriteriaCue does

CriteriaCue helps recruiters review authorized professional profiles against saved job requirements. It supports individual LinkedIn profiles and Chenglietong candidate details, English and Simplified Chinese, and up to 30 locally saved roles, with one to three active at a time. V2 uses an email account and a platform AI service, currently free to registered users. You do not provide an AI API key. Detailed analysis can provide a recommendation reference score, highlighted professional strengths, explicit gaps and items to verify. This score is advisory and is not a calibrated probability. AI output assists human review and does not automatically rank, reject, hire or contact candidates.

Accounts and login email

We store your email address, account ID and creation time in our application database. Resend sends one-time login codes from auth.minus.team. Codes expire after 10 minutes; our database stores a code hash, expiry, attempt count and used status. Old code records are cleaned up when new codes are requested. We store a hash of the login token on the server; the extension stores its login token locally on your browser. A login is valid for 30 days and renews during active use. Signing out revokes that session. Email is used for authentication, service access and usage protection, not automatic marketing enrollment.

Roles and job descriptions

Role titles, original job descriptions (JDs), your edited requirements, language and preferences are stored in local Chrome extension storage, not synced by CriteriaCue. Creating a role from a JD sends the original JD and interface language through our backend to the AI provider. Detailed analysis sends the saved role, original JD, final requirements and currently read professional information. Your final edited requirements guide that analysis. Preset sample roles are fictional and do not require an AI call to load.

Professional profile information

LinkedIn and Chenglietong each require a separate optional Chrome website permission. While the side panel is open, CriteriaCue reads professional sections of the current supported profile. LinkedIn capture progresses as you scroll through basic professional information, About, Experience and Education. Chenglietong reads the loaded professional profile sections once stable, including available work, projects, education, qualifications, languages, skills and self-evaluation. Missing education does not imply that the candidate lacks education.

Professional text can include a name, headline, general location, employers, work history and education. Chenglietong age, gender, salary, contact details and non-professional header fields are excluded from model inputs. We do not use device geolocation, read messages or login credentials, open other candidate profiles, scroll or expand sections for you, or collect general browsing history. Profile URLs and reading boundaries are used locally to validate the current profile and prevent stale or duplicate processing; the profile URL is not part of model inputs.

AI processing and service providers

Quick review sends the read professional text and final requirements to our backend for processing. Requested detailed analysis sends the saved JD, final requirements and read professional text. These requests pass over HTTPS through the MINUS backend hosted by Railway to Alibaba Cloud Bailian. The backend and PostgreSQL database are in Singapore; the currently configured Bailian API uses a Beijing-region endpoint. The decision model performs quick checks; a language model extracts role requirements and produces detailed analysis. Responses are treated as data, not executable code.

Our application database stores encrypted diagnostic copies of platform request and response bodies as described below. Profile URLs, credentials and authentication headers are not intentionally included in these records. Hosting, email and AI providers may process connection, delivery and operational metadata under their own terms. We do not promise that external providers have zero retention or that all processing takes place in Singapore. See Railway, Resend and Alibaba Cloud for their privacy information.

Diagnostic request logs

For all platform accounts, we automatically retain encrypted diagnostic records of submitted AI tasks for 7 days. These records can include the submitted JD, final requirements, captured professional text, actual model prompts and questions, model responses and analysis output, original choices, confidence, probabilities, threshold and prompt-version snapshots, task status and timing. They cover only content already submitted for the requested feature; logging does not expand website access or read additional profiles. Only authorized administrators can view diagnostic content. We use it to troubleshoot responses, assess decision thresholds and service performance, and improve the disclosed review functionality. Optional analytics are separate and do not receive this content.

Diagnostic records become unavailable through the application 7 days after creation. Expired diagnostic rows are physically removed when the service starts and by an hourly cleanup while it is running. Service outages may delay physical cleanup; operational backups can retain copies until replaced or removed. Login codes, authentication tokens, cookies and AI API keys are not intentionally recorded in diagnostics. Non-success upstream HTTP responses are represented by status and error category rather than retaining bodies that could echo credentials. Very large or interrupted responses are marked as incomplete or truncated; failed logging does not automatically repeat an AI call. There are no retroactive diagnostic copies of requests made before logging was enabled. Contact support@minus.team to request deletion of diagnostic records.

Task records and retention

We store task IDs, the account that submitted them, type (role extraction, quick review or detailed analysis), status and creation time for service operation, support and abuse limits. These are required service records, separate from optional analytics. One task can make multiple model requests. Diagnostic model responses may include provider token-usage fields; task counts are not token counts or billing amounts. You can see your recent 24-hour task counts in Settings.

The seven-day diagnostic retention is separate from accounts and basic task metadata, which currently have no fixed automatic deletion schedule. Expired login records are not necessarily deleted immediately; 30 days describes login validity. Contact support@minus.team to request account and associated service-record deletion. Deletion requires an operator process; there is no in-product account deletion button. Operational backups may retain copies until replaced or removed. We cannot recall information already sent to an external provider; related requests must also be handled under that provider's terms.

Your controls

  • Auto-review is on by default. Turn it off in Settings to pause automatic model review. Close the side panel to stop further profile reading.
  • Grant or revoke LinkedIn and Chenglietong access separately. Use only material you are authorized to process.
  • Edit or delete local roles and inspect Profile text. Uninstalling removes extension-local storage but does not delete your server account or task records.
  • Detailed analysis runs when requested and can be cancelled. Cancellation cannot undo processing already completed.
  • Sign out to clear the local login and revoke the current server session.

Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics are off until you actively enable them in Settings and grant the optional PostHog connection permission. PostHog US receives allowlisted feature actions, setup and reading milestones, task timings, fixed error categories, version, bounded counts and a random installation ID used to estimate repeat use. We do not include candidate text or identifiers, profile URLs, role titles or requirements, analysis judgments, credentials, feedback text or arbitrary errors. We do not enable session recording or automatic DOM, click or keyboard capture.

The random ID identifies an installation, not a verified person. It is separate from the email account and is not intentionally joined to candidate data. All core features work with analytics off. Turning analytics off stops new transmissions, cancels pending requests where possible and clears the local ID. Previously received events remain subject to the project's retention settings until expiry or deletion. Copy the Measurement ID before disabling and email support@minus.team to request deletion. There is no persistent local event queue. Network providers necessarily receive IP addresses and connection metadata; analytics handling is subject to PostHog's privacy policy.

Optional feedback

Feedback prepares an email draft or copies it to your clipboard. You review and send it yourself to support@minus.team. It contains your message, feedback type, version and entry point; candidate data, URLs, roles, results and credentials are not attached automatically. Our Tencent Enterprise Email support mailbox receives the sender address and normal email metadata when you send a message. We delete support correspondence within 90 days after the issue closes; you may request earlier deletion. Feedback does not subscribe you to marketing.

Limited use

We use data for the disclosed profile-review functionality, account operation, service protection, quality diagnostics and threshold evaluation, requested support and optional product measurement. We do not sell it or use it for personalized advertising or creditworthiness determinations. CriteriaCue's handling and transfer of user data adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
